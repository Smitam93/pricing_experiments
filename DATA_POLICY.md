# Data provenance and scope

All records in this repository are generated from scratch with a fixed random seed. Entity names, dates, prices, costs, effects and constraints are invented. No employer data, code, screenshots, model parameters, customer records or internal definitions were used. This is an independent educational implementation inspired by broad professional experience, not a reconstruction of an employer system.

Results describe the synthetic environment only. They are not measured employer outcomes, independently verified commercial benchmarks or deployment recommendations. The original resume and recommendation letters are deliberately excluded. Do not add them to this repository.

Before publishing changes, check that every added dataset is synthetic or has a documented public source and redistribution license. Keep real experience and simulated results separate in interviews and resume bullets.
